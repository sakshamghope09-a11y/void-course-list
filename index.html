A LITTLE CLARITY

Questions & answers

How do I unlock a course?+

Tap Unlock Course to open a Telegram chat with @voidx_exee. The selected course enquiry is passed to Telegram as a draft. Tap Send there, then confirm the price, availability and payment details before purchasing.

Where are the prices?+

Prices are intentionally not listed on this website. Every course is unlocked on demand — tap Unlock Course and @voidx_exee will confirm the current price for your chosen course on Telegram.

Can I unlock several courses at once?+

Yes. Tap + Cart on the courses you want, then View cart or Buy all on Telegram. Your full selection is included in the Telegram enquiry. To keep messages manageable, a cart holds up to 15 courses and may hold fewer if course names are very long.

How will I receive course access?+

Confirm the delivery method, timing and access duration directly with us before paying. This website is a catalogue; it does not automatically process payments or deliver course files.

Which payments and refunds are available?+

Ask @voidx_exee for the payment methods and refund terms for your chosen course before purchase. No payment is collected on this website.

What about video quality and downloads?+

Quality, language, download permissions and access duration can vary by course. Please confirm these details on Telegram before purchasing.

Where do the rating numbers come from?+

A numeric rating badge is shown only when a course's rating score and review count have been supplied by the catalogue owner. These badges are not clickable, and this website does not collect reviews. Courses without supplied rating data have no badge.
